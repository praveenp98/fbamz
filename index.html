What it does

One system for ads, inventory and account health

Ads

Daily optimization toward your ACoS target

  • Negatives: cuts terms with clicks and no orders
  • Harvesting: moves winning searches into exact match
  • Bids: small daily steps, capped so nothing swings wildly
  • Budgets: moves money to campaigns that run out by afternoon
Inventory

No more ads driving your best SKU into a stockout

  • Days of cover per SKU, from sales velocity and inbound stock
  • Reorder alerts with a suggested quantity
  • Ads paused automatically when stock runs low
  • Stranded and aged stock flagged before fees pile up
Account guard

Problems caught in hours, not weeks

  • Buy Box loss and new sellers on your listings
  • Price changes and possible hijackers
  • Account health: order defects, late shipments, policy warnings
Control

Your team stays in charge

  • Approvals: big changes wait for one click in Slack or email
  • Guardrails: limits on bid and budget moves, set by you
  • Full log of every change, with an undo
  • Monday report: sales, ACoS, TACoS and what changed

FAQ

Questions brands ask

Is it fully automated from day one?

No, on purpose. Week 1 is read-only so you see every recommendation first. From week 2, routine changes apply within limits you set, and anything bigger waits for your approval. A person works alongside your team for the first 30 days.

What access do you need?

A user login on Seller Central (inventory, advertising and reports) and Editor access on the Amazon Ads console. Later, you approve our app through Amazon's official authorization so the system can connect directly. You can remove access at any time.

Does it replace my team?

It replaces the repetitive part of the job. Your team keeps strategy, launches, creative and supplier relationships, and gets their mornings back.

Which marketplaces do you support?

Amazon US, for Seller Central (3P) brands using FBA. Other marketplaces on request.

What does it cost?

A one-time setup fee, which includes 30 days of hands-on onboarding, plus monthly support for 12 months. The setup is sized to your account, usually well under a quarter of a full-time hire's first-year cost. Ask for a quote by email.

Are you part of Amazon?

No. FBAmz is an independent service that works with Amazon's official seller and advertising tools. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Amazon.
